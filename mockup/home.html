How we work

From the farm gate to your store

We handle every step between the farmer and the buyer, so you get grain you can rely on.

  1. STEP 1

    Buy from farmers

    We buy maize, soybeans and other grains directly from smallholder farmers in [districts] and pay fair, prompt prices.

  2. STEP 2

    Clean & grade

    Every batch is dried, cleaned and checked for moisture and quality before it goes into storage.

  3. STEP 3

    Store safely

    Grain is bagged and kept in a secure, dry warehouse to protect it from pests and spoilage.

  4. STEP 4

    Deliver to buyers

    We supply processors, feed mills, schools and traders, in bulk or by the bag, when they need it.
